# Product Company Interview Preparation

A practical preparation programme for an experienced Java/backend engineer targeting senior software engineering roles at product companies, including FAANG-level companies.

## Candidate profile and strategy
- Experience: approximately 13 years in software engineering, including SAP Labs and enterprise commerce/integration platforms.
- Primary language: Java.
- Current coding confidence: rusty; rebuild fundamentals through deliberate, independent implementation.
- Daily commitment: 3 hours, six focused days per week.
- Target roles: Senior Software Engineer, Senior Backend Engineer, Lead Software Engineer; selectively Staff/Architect roles where scope and interview expectations fit.
- Target market: FAANG and strong product companies.
- AI positioning: AI-enabled backend engineer who can design and ship reliable AI features, not a machine-learning researcher.

## Study allocation (180 minutes)
| Area | Daily time | Priority |
|---|---:|---|
| DSA and coding in Java | 60 min | Highest |
| Java and Spring/backend fundamentals | 35 min | High |
| HLD/LLD system design | 35 min | High |
| AI engineering | 35 min | Strategic differentiator |
| Revision, applications or interview journal | 15 min | Consistency |

Use six study days each week. On the seventh day, rest or take a light 30-minute review/mock assessment. If work is busy, preserve at least 45 minutes of DSA and 30 minutes of design rather than abandoning the routine.

## How to study
1. Attempt coding problems without AI first. Explain the approach, complexity and edge cases aloud.
2. Use AI only after an honest attempt: ask for hints, review, test cases or alternative approaches. Re-type and explain the final solution yourself.
3. Keep a mistake log: concept gap, missed edge case, complexity issue, implementation bug or communication issue.
4. Re-solve important problems after 1, 7 and 21 days without looking at the solution.
5. Build one production-style backend project and one AI-enabled project; keep scope small and finish them.
6. Start applying before the 12 weeks are complete. Preparation and job search should run in parallel.

## Weekly success measures
- 8–12 quality DSA problems attempted, with previous problems re-solved.
- One Java/backend topic revised with runnable examples.
- One design exercise completed or improved.
- One AI engineering concept implemented or tested.
- One mock interview or verbal explanation every week from Week 4.
- Track applications, referrals and interview feedback in TRACKER.md.
